Forensic Coordinate & Directionality Convention:
  • Calibration Requirement: Only measurements calibrated in physical millimeters (mm/px) are eligible. Uncalibrated pixel dimensions are strictly blocked from spatial reconstruction.
  • Direction of Travel (γ ∈ [0°, 360°)): Must be supplied by the examiner from physical satellite tail/spine elongation (measured counter-clockwise from +X_surface). Reverse source bearing is derived as β = (γ + 180°) mod 360°.
  • Linear Stringing Limitation: Calculated intersections neglect gravity and aerodynamic drag and represent an approximate 2D Area of Convergence and 3D Area of Origin, never an exact point source.

🔬 Scientific Principles & Geometric Foundation

When an airborne spherical blood droplet strikes an inclined target surface, forward momentum elongates the deposit along the direction of travel, creating an elliptical bloodstain.

sin(α) = Opposite / Hypotenuse = Width / Length
α = arcsin(Width / Length)

• Width (W): Minor axis (corresponds to initial spherical droplet diameter).
• Length (L): Major axis (corresponds to elongated impact travel, excluding spines and satellite spatter).
• Physical Boundary: The minor axis can never exceed the major axis (W ≤ L).

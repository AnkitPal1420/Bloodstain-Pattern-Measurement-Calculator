Scientific & Forensic Model Limitation (Straight-Line Stringing Approximation):
Back-projected rays model linear trajectories (Z = D × tan(α)) and neglect gravitational acceleration and aerodynamic drag, which systematically overestimates the true source height (Z). An elliptical stain alone has a 180° directional ambiguity; direction of travel (γ) must be supplied from physical tail/spine morphology. Calculated intersections represent an approximate 2D Area of Convergence and 3D Area of Origin with uncertainty bounds, never an exact point source.

⚠️ Mandatory Scientific Limitations on Overlapping Stain Reconstruction

  • Examiner-Assisted Decision Support Only: Candidate watershed cut lines and child stain boundaries are preliminary mathematical suggestions requiring visual verification.
  • Absence of 3D Temporal Deposition Evidence: 2D photograph segmentation cannot determine droplet deposition order or vertical layering (which stain struck first/second) without macroscopic drying rims, differential clotting, or biochemical analysis.
  • Merged Region Integrity: Merged stains with unresolved boundaries MUST NOT be used for trigonometric impact angle back-projection or 3D trajectory convergence.
  • Area Conservation & Edge Disparity: Discrepancies between parent contour area and child areas due to boundary erosion are explicitly recorded and tracked.

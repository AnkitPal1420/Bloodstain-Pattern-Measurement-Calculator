Forensic Methodology Notice: Impact angle estimates ($\theta = \arcsin(W/L)$) assume an idealized elliptical droplet on a flat surface. Analytical error propagation utilizes first-order Taylor expansion partial derivatives ($\partial\theta/\partial W$, $\partial\theta/\partial L$); Monte Carlo simulation models parameter dispersion across $N$ draws. Neither simulation intervals nor analytical error bounds represent an absolute physical probability of incident deposition. Isotropic pixel calibration cancels in the dimensionless ratio $W/L$, meaning scale uncertainty affects physical dimensions but not the calculated angle.
